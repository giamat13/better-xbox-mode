Controls

  • Xbox button App switcher
  • D-pad / Left stick Navigate  ·  move the mouse on the desktop
  • A Select  ·  left click
  • B Back  ·  Esc
  • X Close app  ·  right click
  • Y On-screen keyboard (desktop)
  • LB / RB Switch tab
  • Menu Settings
  • Right stick Scroll  ·  sideways scrolls horizontally  ·  click it to jump to the other monitor
  • LB (desktop) Copy, then open the clipboard history
  • LT / RT (desktop) Zoom the screen out / in  ·  both resets
  • Hold Xbox button Quick settings
  • Menu in the switcher Window options: maximize, snap, move to another monitor
  • Y in the switcher Controller mode of that app: game (it keeps the controller) or mouse
